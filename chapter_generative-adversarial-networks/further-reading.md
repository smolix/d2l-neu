# Further Reading
:label:`sec_gan-further-reading`

This section connects the core concepts of the chapter (density-ratio estimation, divergence formulations, relativistic objectives, gradient penalties, and adversarial post-training) to the broader literature and open-source implementations.

## Recommended Entry Points and Codebases

1. **Local Convergence and Gradient Penalties:** :citet:`Mescheder.Geiger.Nowozin.2018` prove the local convergence theorem cited in :numref:`sec_gan_convergence`, showing that zero-centered gradient penalties ($R_1$ and $R_2$) stabilize alternating gradient updates near equilibrium where weight clipping and one-centered penalties (WGAN-GP) fail. Their [GAN stability repository](https://github.com/LMescheder/GAN_stability) includes implementations of the Dirac-GAN and $R_1$/$R_2$ regularizers.
2. **Modernized GAN Baseline (R3GAN):** :citet:`Huang.Gokaslan.Kuleshov.ea.2024` combine the relativistic pairing loss with $R_1 + R_2$ penalties and modern convolutional blocks, showing that a well-regularized objective eliminates many of the ad-hoc architectural stabilizers of earlier GANs ([official R3GAN repository](https://github.com/brownvc/R3GAN)).
3. **Evaluation Metrics (Precision, Recall, FID, KID):** :citet:`Kynkaanniemi.Karras.Laine.ea.2019` introduce the $k$-NN precision and recall metrics of :numref:`subsec_gan_precision_recall`, separating sample fidelity from mode coverage. The [StyleGAN3 repository](https://github.com/NVlabs/stylegan3) :cite:`Karras.Aittala.Laine.ea.2021` provides reference implementations of full-scale FID, KID, precision, and recall.
4. **Adversarial Diffusion Distillation:** DMD2 :cite:`Yin.Gharbi.Park.ea.2024` combines distribution-matching score distillation with a discriminator trained against real images, enabling a one-step student generator to outperform its multi-step diffusion teacher.
5. **Interactive Visualization and Historical Surveys:** The [NIPS 2016 tutorial](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.00160) :cite:`Goodfellow.2016` and the [ganhacks checklist](https://github.com/soumith/ganhacks) capture early GAN training heuristics, while [GAN Lab](https://poloclub.github.io/ganlab/) :cite:`Kahng.Thorat.Chau.ea.2018` visualizes 2D generator and discriminator dynamics in the browser. In a large-scale benchmark, :citet:`Lucic.Kurach.Michalski.ea.2018` demonstrate that many adversarial objectives (:numref:`sec_gan_objectives`) achieve comparable FID when given equal hyperparameter tuning budgets.

## Objectives and Minimax Dynamics

- **Least-Squares GAN (LSGAN) Coding Schemes:** :numref:`sec_gan_objectives` showed that the 0–1 least-squares discriminator evaluates $\tfrac14 \chi^2(p \,\|\, m)$. :citet:`Mao.Li.Xie.ea.2017` analyze general target labels $b$ (real), $a$ (fake), and $c$ (generator target), showing that at the optimal critic the generator objective reduces to $\tfrac14 (a + b - 2c)^2 + \tfrac14 (b - a)^2 \chi^2(p \,\|\, m)$—a positive multiple of the same $\chi^2$ divergence for any $b \neq a$.
- **Output Activations for $f$-GANs:** To ensure the critic output $t$ lies in the domain where the convex conjugate $f^*(t)$ is finite (:numref:`sec_gan_objectives`), :citet:`Nowozin.Cseke.Tomioka.2016` apply domain-specific output activations $t(v)$ to the raw network output $v$: identity for forward KL, $-e^{-v}$ for reverse KL ($t < 0$), and $\log 2 - \mathrm{softplus}(-v)$ for Jensen–Shannon ($t < \log 2$).
- **General Relativistic Divergences:** :citet:`Jolicoeur-Martineau.2020` proves that the calibrated optimal value of the relativistic pairing objective (:numref:`sec_gan_relativistic`) defines a valid divergence for any concave payoff $\ell$ satisfying $\ell(0) = 0$, $\ell'(0) \neq 0$, and $\sup_{t > 0} \ell(t) > 0$.
- **Optimization Algorithms for Bilinear Minimax Problems:** On the bilinear problem $\min_\theta \max_\psi \theta\psi$, simultaneous gradient descent of step size $h$ amplifies the distance to the origin by $\sqrt{1 + h^2}$ per step. The *extragradient* method :cite:`Korpelevich.1976` evaluates the gradient at a lookahead trial point, contracting the distance by $\sqrt{1 - h^2 + h^4} < 1$ for $0 < h < 1$. *Optimistic gradient descent* :cite:`Daskalakis.Ilyas.Syrgkanis.ea.2018` approximates the lookahead using the previous step's gradient, and negative momentum :cite:`Gidel.Askari-Hemmat.Pezeshki.ea.2019` stabilizes alternating updates. While these methods modify the optimizer, large-scale image GANs instead modify the objective via $R_1$/$R_2$ gradient penalties (:numref:`sec_gan_convergence`).

## Fixed versus Learned Discrepancies

Can a generator be trained directly against a fixed two-sample distance without a critic network? Generative Moment Matching Networks :cite:`Li.Swersky.Zemel.2015` minimize fixed-kernel MMD (:numref:`sec_gan_objectives`), Implicit Maximum Likelihood Estimation (IMLE) :cite:`Li.Malik.2018` matches each real example to its nearest generated sample to prevent mode dropping, and Sliced Wasserstein generators :cite:`Deshpande.Zhang.Schwing.2018` average 1D optimal transport distances across random projections. On natural images, however, fixed pixel-space kernels and random projections struggle to capture perceptual differences, motivating adversarial kernel learning in MMD-GAN :cite:`Li.Chang.Cheng.ea.2017,Binkowski.Sutherland.Arbel.ea.2018`.

## Architectures, Feature Spaces, and Data Efficiency

- **Architectural Evolution (2015–2021):** Between DCGAN :cite:`Radford.Metz.Chintala.2015` and R3GAN :cite:`Huang.Gokaslan.Kuleshov.ea.2024`, architectural advances addressed training stability and high-resolution synthesis: progressive growing :cite:`Karras.Aila.Laine.ea.2017`, spectral normalization and self-attention in SAGAN and BigGAN :cite:`Miyato.Kataoka.Koyama.ea.2018,Zhang.Goodfellow.Metaxas.ea.2019,Brock.Donahue.Simonyan.2019`, and StyleGAN's latent mapping network, style modulation/demodulation, and alias-free resampling :cite:`Karras.Laine.Aila.2019,Karras.Laine.Aittala.ea.2020,Karras.Aittala.Laine.ea.2021`.
- **Feature Spaces and Metric Pitfalls:** :citet:`Kynkaanniemi.Karras.Aittala.ea.2023` show that Inception-v3 features are heavily shaped by ImageNet class boundaries, whereas self-supervised DINOv2 features align better with human perceptual judgments :cite:`Stein.Cresswell.Hosseinzadeh.ea.2023,Karras.Aittala.Kynkaanniemi.ea.2024`. In addition, aliased image resizing can distort FID :cite:`Parmar.Zhang.Zhu.2022`, and finite-sample FID bias scales as $O(1/n)$ with a model-dependent slope :cite:`Chong.Forsyth.2020`.
- **Pretrained Features in the Critic:** Projected GAN :cite:`Sauer.Chitta.Muller.ea.2021` and StyleGAN-T :cite:`Sauer.Karras.Laine.ea.2023` apply lightweight discriminator heads on top of frozen pretrained vision backbones, accelerating convergence; when doing so, evaluation should always use an independent feature extractor.
- **Amortizing Gradient Penalties:** Because $R_1$ and $R_2$ require second-order backpropagation through the critic, StyleGAN2 evaluates the penalty only once every 16 minibatches (*lazy regularization*, :citet:`Karras.Laine.Aittala.ea.2020`), while large video diffusion post-training approximates $R_1$ via finite differences under small Gaussian input perturbations :cite:`Lin.Xia.Ren.ea.2025`.
- **Non-Leaking Data Augmentation:** On small datasets, critics rapidly memorize training images (:numref:`sec_dcgan`). Adaptive Discriminator Augmentation (ADA) :cite:`Karras.Aittala.Hellsten.ea.2020` and DiffAugment :cite:`Zhao.Liu.Lin.ea.2020` apply invertible, *non-leaking* transformations to both real and generated images inside the gradient path, preventing critic overfitting without corrupting the target distribution.

## Conditioning, Translation, and Modern Hybrid Systems

- **Feature Modulation Layers:** The channelwise affine modulation $a(c) \odot h + b(c)$ in :numref:`sec_gan_conditional` appears as Conditional Batch Normalization :cite:`De-Vries.Strub.Mary.ea.2017`, FiLM :cite:`Perez.Strub.De-Vries.ea.2018`, Adaptive Instance Normalization (AdaIN) for style transfer :cite:`Huang.Belongie.2017`, and spatially varying SPADE for semantic image synthesis :cite:`Park.Liu.Wang.ea.2019`.
- **Paired and Unpaired Image-to-Image Translation:** Pix2pix :cite:`Isola.Zhu.Zhou.ea.2017` pairs a U-Net generator with a patch-level discriminator (PatchGAN) to sharpen $L_1$ reconstructions. CycleGAN :cite:`Zhu.Park.Isola.ea.2017` extends translation to unpaired domains by enforcing cycle consistency ($F(G(x)) \approx x$), though cycle-consistent maps can hide source information in high-frequency steganographic perturbations when domains differ in entropy :cite:`Chu.Zhmoginov.Sandler.2017`.
- **Tokenizers, Few-Step Distillation, and Vocoders:** In visual tokenizers (VQGAN, ViTok), adversarial decoder losses trade pixelwise PSNR for perceptual reconstruction quality :cite:`Esser.Rombach.Ommer.2021,Hansen-Estruch.Yan.Chung.ea.2025,Hansen-Estruch.Chen.Ramanujan.ea.2026`. In diffusion distillation, LADD :cite:`Sauer.Boesel.Dockhorn.ea.2024` (used in FLUX.1-schnell, :citet:`BlackForestLabs.2024`) and SDXL-Lightning :cite:`Lin.Wang.Yang.2024` apply discriminators directly in latent or patch space, while MeanFlow :cite:`Geng.Deng.Bai.ea.2025` and continuous-time consistency models :cite:`Lu.Song.2025` explore critic-free few-step regression objectives. In speech synthesis, RAF :cite:`Lee.Choi.2026` upgrades BigVGAN :cite:`Lee.Ping.Ginsburg.ea.2023` with the relativistic pairing objective of :numref:`sec_gan_relativistic`.
- **Likelihood Models as Implicit Critics (DDO):** Direct Discriminative Optimization :cite:`Zheng.Chen.Chen.ea.2025` fine-tunes a likelihood or diffusion model $q_\theta$ against a frozen reference copy $q_{\textrm{ref}}$ by parameterizing the critic itself as the log-likelihood ratio $D_\theta(x) = \beta \log(q_\theta(x) / q_{\textrm{ref}}(x))$, eliminating a separate discriminator network (Exercise :exref:`ex_adversarial-losses-ddo-plays-log-loss`).
