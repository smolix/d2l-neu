**Idea:** A softmax head must put its mass somewhere, and each fix for the mass it does not need (a sink, a gate, a window, a partial or absent position) trades something that can be measured at small scale.
**Opening:** Most heads of Qwen3-0.6B dump a third to half of their mass on the first token; Gemma 4 E2B sees only 512 tokens in most of its layers and works at a 128K context. The concrete example is the opening probe: first-token mass, maximum-to-median residual magnitude and the fraction of near-zero gate values for Qwen3-1.7B, Qwen3.5-2B and Gemma 4 E2B on the same 2K-token passages, with the disclaimer that differences between released models are pointers, not ablations. The open question: which of these fixes still matters once a model is trained with it from the start?
**Teaches:**
- Sinks: a head with nothing to attend to cannot output zero, since the output lies in the convex hull of the values, so it parks its mass on a token whose value is nearly zero; massive activations are the mechanism; a learned sink logit (gpt-oss) is exactly one extra key with logit s and zero value, and the output is the standard one scaled by sigma(LSE minus s).
- Gates: an elementwise sigmoid gate on the attention output (Qwen3.5's doubled `q_proj`, 8 heads of 256) lets a head emit zero without a sink and adds H d_h d parameters per layer; the T0 toy shows the baseline concentrating, the gate spreading, the sink using its column.
- Positions: partial RoPE (rotate the first fraction of the head's channels; the static pairs are those whose wavelength exceeds the context, 10 of 32 for Qwen3.5-2B at theta 1e7 and 262,144) and interleaved NoPE layers (SmolLM3-3B drops the rotation in every fourth layer) beyond the training length, measured by pure extrapolation without scaling (so that the measurement is of the architecture, not of YaRN, which is 4.5's).
- Local-global layouts as a KV dial: with a fraction f_g of global layers and window w, the cache is 2 H_kv d_h b L (f_g n plus (1 minus f_g) min(n, w)); Gemma 4's windows of 512 (E2B, E4B) and 1,024 (12B, 26B-A4B, 31B) with the ratio read from `layer_types`; gpt-oss's alternating 128-token windows (36,864 growing bytes per token against 4.7 MB bounded). Per-layer embeddings (Gemma 4 E models) in one paragraph: a per-layer input table that buys capacity with memory rather than FLOPs. Differential attention in one sentence.
**Derivation:** A learned sink logit s equals one extra key of logit s and zero value (`eq_lm_sink_equiv`); the sink margin: a zero-value key whose logit exceeds the others by at least ln(nV over epsilon) bounds the output norm by epsilon (`eq_lm_sink_margin`), and the minimum output norm without a sink is the distance from the origin to the convex hull of the values (`eq_lm_sink_hull`). Smallest case: logits (2, 1, 0) with a sink at 3, where the three standard weights are scaled by sigma(LSE minus 3). Check cells: `d2l.attention_with_sink` against a concatenated softmax (exact) and against sigma(LSE minus s) times SDPA (1e-6); the margin bisected for n equal to 2^k at k from 1 to 14 with slope 1 in ln n; the partial-rotary static-pair count against brute force for five released configurations (exact integers).

**Writer notes:**
- Differences from the blueprint: Gemma 4 replaces Gemma 3 as the local-global exemplar (windows 512 and 1,024, 128K and 256K contexts, Apache-2.0; the ratio from `layer_types`, since the card states none); per-layer embeddings get one paragraph and the open exercise; differential attention is one sentence with its citation; gpt-oss-20b is optional (about 42 GB dequantized on an A100, no MXFP4 kernels there; a T2 pair or skip).
- The sink is not necessarily the first token (`sink_metrics` measures position 0 only), and removing the first token moves the mass onto refused tokens. "Gated attention" (this section's output gate) and "gated DeltaNet" (14.6's decay and write gates) are different mechanisms; name both. The unrotated channels of partial RoPE are not NoPE heads; a NoPE layer drops the rotation for the whole layer. The book's `_rope` rotates adjacent pairs while Transformers rotates half-split; the loader of 1.2 permutes, `RefinedAttention` carries the flag and a permutation test, and the partial rotary applies to the first `rope_frac` times `head_dim` channels. Extrapolation without scaling measures the architecture, not YaRN.
- Fallbacks: the reference recipe differs from the papers', so compare only within the notebook; effects below noise give ties plus diagnostics plus the T0 toy; fused sink kernels on A100 and Ada are unconfirmed (use the LSE-gate identity or an explicit fp32 softmax); if copy accuracy is near zero, score the loss on the second copy or train longer.
- Library additions: `d2l.RefinedAttention(d, num_heads, num_kv_heads, head_dim, *, rope=True, rope_frac=1.0, rope_base=10000.0, window=None, qk_norm=True, sink=False, gate=None)` (gate in `sigmoid` from a doubled query projection as in Qwen3.5, `swish`, or none; reused by 14.6 for Qwen3.5's full-attention layers); `d2l.attention_with_sink(q, k, v, sink, mask)`; `d2l.refined_gpt(...)` with `nope_every`, `window_every`; `d2l.sink_metrics(model, batch)`; `d2l.repeat_copy_task`, `d2l.eval_copy`; `d2l.diff_attention(q1, k1, q2, k2, v, lam)` (one sentence in prose, a check cell only).
