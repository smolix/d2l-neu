**Idea:** A released hybrid is the Advanced recipe, one attention layer in a few, at scale, and its serving cost follows from the attention fraction and the fixed state of the linear layers.
**Opening:** The `sec_hybrids` model had two layers; Qwen3.5-2B has gated DeltaNet in three positions of four and gated attention in the fourth, 18 linear and 6 full layers. The concrete example is the opening cell listing the layer counts of released hybrids read from their configurations (Qwen3-Next, Qwen3.5-2B and 35B-A3B, Qwen3.6-27B, Qwen3.8-2.4T-A95B, Kimi Linear's 20 KDA and 7 MLA, Kimi K3's 69 and 24, MiniMax-M1's 70 lightning and 10 softmax, GLM-5.3-Flash's 34 KDA and 11 sparse, Nemotron's `hybrid_override_pattern`, and MiniMax-M2's 62 of 62 softmax as the reminder that the choice is not monotone). The open question: where does the fixed state stop paying?
**Teaches:**
- The released recipe: where the attention layers sit (`full_attention_interval` 4 in the Qwen line; never first), what the linear layer contains in practice (a depthwise causal convolution over the projected q, k, v, L2-normalized queries and keys, a decay gate from a softplus with a learned log-scale, a write gate beta from a sigmoid, head replication when value heads outnumber key heads, a gated RMSNorm before the output projection), and what the chunked kernel does: a unit-lower-triangular solve per chunk with gamma ratios kept in log space.
- Loading Qwen3.5-2B into the book's code: `d2l.Qwen35Hybrid` composed from the book's `d2l.GatedDeltaNet` (extended by the pieces above in a subclass) and `d2l.Qwen3Attention` with 14.4's output gate and partial RoPE, with the mismatch table (`tab_lm_hy_mapping`: the (1 plus w) norm convention folded at load, the gated norm applied before the SiLU gate, SwiGLU at width 6,144, the doubled query projection split into query and gate, half-split RoPE on the first 64 of 256 channels at theta 1e7, the vision tower and MTP head skipped by name prefix) and a layer-by-layer logit match.
- Cache bytes at context L for attention fraction f: M(n) equals L_f c_kv n plus L_l c_st with the fixed state c_st equal to H_v d_k d_v b_s plus the convolution state, the crossover n-star where the state equals the cache, and the ratio to an attention-only peer; prefix caching needs state snapshots, and a cached state cannot be truncated or extended backwards (13.4).
- Relatives in one difference each: Qwen3.6 and Qwen3.8 (the same layout at 27B dense and 35B-A3B or 2.4T-A95B MoE), Kimi Linear and K3 (KDA with a lower-bounded, full-rank gate and NoPE MLA in the attention layers), Nemotron 3 (Mamba-2 plus MoE, trained in NVFP4), Mamba-3 (trapezoidal discretization, complex state, multi-input heads; the layer belongs to `chap_modern_rnn`, which gets the same pointer).
**Derivation:** The gated chunked delta rule (`eq_lm_hy_chunked`): the recurrence S_t equals a_t(I minus beta_t k_t k_t-transposed)S_(t minus 1) plus beta_t k_t v_t-transposed is computed chunk-wise exactly by one triangular solve, with outputs from the chunk-start state plus a masked intra-chunk term; and the state-against-cache arithmetic (`eq_lm_hy_state`). Smallest case: Qwen3.5-2B with 6 full layers at 2,048 bytes per token and 18 linear layers at 1,085,440 bytes of state each gives M(n) equal to 12,288 n plus 19,537,920, n-star about 1,590, and 9.3 times less than Qwen3-1.7B at long context; at 128K, Qwen3.5-35B-A3B holds 2.75 GB per sequence against 12.88 GB for Qwen3-30B-A3B, about 4.7 times less. Check cells: chunked against recurrent in fp64 (1e-10 relative for chunks of 16, 64, 256); both against Transformers' `torch_chunk_gated_delta_rule` and `torch_recurrent_gated_delta_rule` in fp32 (1e-5); against `fla`'s kernel when it imports (tolerance measured); the gate at zero reduces to the book's `chunked_delta_rule` (1e-6); `HybridCache.bytes()` after an n-token prefill equals M(n) exactly on the loaded model.

**Writer notes:**
- Library naming: the export is `d2l.Qwen35Hybrid`, a `d2l.Qwen3GPT` subclass whose blocks are built from `d2l.Qwen3Attention` (with the 14.4 gate and partial RoPE) and a `d2l.GatedDeltaNet` subclass carrying Qwen3.5's extras; the blueprint's `GatedDeltaNetLayer`, `hybrid_gpt` and `load_qwen35` are the same objects under the names used here (`d2l.load_qwen35` stays as the loader). The book's classes are unchanged (the 1.2 pattern). State this in the interface contract (`01_interfaces.md` gains the export).
- T3 model: the T3 served hybrid is Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (Apache-2.0, released 2026-04-15, the same `qwen3_5_moe_text` layout) against Qwen3-30B-A3B; the blueprint's Qwen3.5-35B-A3B is the fallback row; recompute the capacity pilot (about 20 sequences of 128K for the hybrid against 8 for the peer at 36 GB per GPU). Engine support for the Qwen3.5 hybrid cache at the pinned version is unverified; the fallback is Transformers with `fla` in static batches, reporting the first three metrics only; engine page granularity inflates state bytes, so the exact-byte assertion holds on `HybridCache` only.
- The recurrent state is per sequence, not per token, and is not small (1.09 MB per Qwen3.5-2B linear layer in fp32; it exceeds the KV cache below n-star). Linear-layer FLOPs are constant in n but not small, so short-context throughput may be lower. Two norm conventions live in one layer (the (1 plus w) RMSNorm and the unscaled gated norm); fold per tensor. `A_log`, the linear norm weight and the state stay in fp32. If the fp32 difference on the 0.8B model exceeds 1e-3, localize the first diverging layer before touching tolerances.
- `fla`'s Triton kernels run on A100 and Ada in principle; confirm in the notebook, and label the plot "PyTorch chunked only" if they do not import. Transformers' own `Qwen3_5GatedDeltaNet` uses `fla` and `causal-conv1d` when present and PyTorch functions otherwise; the notebook's code is the oracle. Qwen3.8-Flash-Next's layout (mHC, an indexer, n-gram tables) is not loadable into this class and stays a table row. Request the Mamba-3 pointer in `chap_modern_rnn`'s Further Reading (book-side edit).
