**Idea:** A low-rank latent for keys and values can be absorbed into the query and output projections at decode, so the cache holds one small vector per token, if and only if the rotary position is kept out of the absorbed path.
**Opening:** The GQA cache of Qwen3-8B still stores eight heads of keys and values per layer, 147,456 bytes per token; a single latent of width 512 per token would cut that to 1,152 bytes but seems to cost a re-expansion at every step. The concrete example is the opening arithmetic: DeepSeek-V3's expanded keys and values would be 4,997,120 bytes per token over 61 layers; its latent is 70,272, 71 times less and 2.1 times less than Qwen3-8B's GQA. The open question: can an existing GQA model be converted?
**Teaches:**
- MLA: a down-projection to a latent c of rank r with a decoupled rotary slice of width d_r, up-projections for keys and values, and the absorption of the key up-projection into the query and of the value up-projection into the output projection, which turns decode into multi-query attention with one head of width r plus d_r; absorption is a reordering, never a pre-merge (merging W_O with W_UV is 3.7 times larger).
- Why RoPE blocks absorption: after the up-projection the rotation sits between the two factors and depends on the position difference, so the absorbed query would be position-dependent; the decoupled slice carries the rotation separately at a cost of d_r over r plus d_r (64 of 576, about 11%).
- Decode cost per context token per layer and arithmetic intensity for three strategies (expanded cache, latent re-expanded each step at about 120 times the FLOPs, absorbed); prefill is a different trade (absorbed costs about 3.4 times the attention FLOPs of expanded, so prefill runs expanded).
- Cross-layer sharing (one cache read by adjacent layers; the borrower rotates its queries against the owner's keys) and conversion of a trained GQA checkpoint by a joint, optionally activation-aware, SVD of its key and value projections with a short recovery.
**Derivation:** The absorption identity (`eq_lm_mla_absorb`) and why a rotation between the factors breaks it (`eq_lm_mla_rope`); the activation-aware joint low-rank factorization (`eq_lm_mla_awsvd`, a whitening by the Cholesky factor of X-transposed X, reducing to a data-free joint SVD at X equal to I). Smallest case: two heads at dimension 8 with latent 4 in prose; in code d 256, H 8, d_n 32, d_r 16, d_v 32, r 64, r_q 96, seeded, where explicit and absorbed agree to 1e-10 in fp64 and 1e-5 relative in fp32, the three strategies agree, `MLACache.bytes()` equals L(r plus d_r) times bytes times n, RoPE after the up-projection against position-ignorant absorption differs by a relative error of order one, and `config_stats(DeepSeek-V3).cache_bytes_per_token` is exactly 70,272.

**Writer notes:**
- The three traps in order: latent caching with re-expansion costs about 120 times the absorbed FLOPs (33.6M against 278,528 per context token per layer at V3 dimensions); absorption is reordering, not pre-merging; absorbed intensity about 242 FLOP per byte at 128 heads makes decode compute-bound on bandwidth-rich chips (K2 with 64 heads sits lower). Validate in fp32 and fp64 before bf16, where the absorbed chain is longer and the error larger.
- Field semantics: `num_key_value_heads` is a compatibility field for MLA; `mla_use_nope` keeps the rope slots (576 numbers per token even in Kimi K3), read the shapes; conversion without recovery is a capability probe, not a result; a cross-layer borrower must rotate queries consistently with the owner's keys.
- Hardware: SDPA has no fused kernel for a 576-wide head on Ada, so the measurement uses the math or efficient backend and says "unfused"; FlashMLA's 2026-09-30 release dropped Hopper and targets Blackwell and Ascend, so measured latencies are upper bounds; if the `chip_specs()` row is missing, measure bandwidth with a large copy and FLOPs with a large matmul; the expanded arm at batch 16 and n 32K may not fit (drop the cell); Transformers' `DeepseekV3Attention` signature may change (small config, pinned version).
- Difference from the blueprint: the conversion target is Qwen3-0.6B (the book's own checkpoint) with GPT-2 as the no-RoPE control, instead of GPT-2 alone; the partial-RoPE handling is decided at authoring (keep the top pairs per MHA2MLA, or convert in NoPE form and recover).
- Library additions: `d2l.MLAAttention(d, num_heads, dn, dr, dv, r, rq=None, *, rope_base=10000.0, nope=False)` with `forward(x, cache=None, mode='expanded'|'absorbed', pos=0)`; `d2l.MLACache` (`append`, `bytes()`); `d2l.mla_decode_cost(H, dn, dr, dv, r, n, *, strategy, bytes_per=2) -> dict(flops, bytes, intensity)`; `d2l.CrossLayerKV(share)` and `d2l.cla_gpt(vocab, d, H, Hkv, L, share)`; `d2l.joint_svd_kv(W_k, W_v, r, *, calib=None)`; `d2l.mha_to_mla(attn, r, *, calib=None)`.
