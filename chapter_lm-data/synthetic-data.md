**Idea:** Rephrasing trades diversity for quality, and the trade is measurable at equal tokens.
**Opening:** A small model turns one messy web page into a clean encyclopedic paragraph that keeps every number. The reader wants to train on a million such paragraphs. The section measures, at an equal token budget, what rephrased text adds to and takes from the original, and closes on who may train on generated text at all.
**Teaches:**
- Rephrasing web text in two styles with a fixed prompt (`d2l.REPHRASE_PROMPTS`, `d2l.rephrase_docs`) and textbook-style generation from a seed as its relative (new content, not a rewrite); fidelity checks (copy rate, number fidelity, length ratio, `d2l.synth_metrics`) and diversity (`d2l.distinct_n`, near-duplicate rate with 3.3's tools).
- Quality against diversity as the one trade: five arms at equal tokens (original repeated, original plus rephrased, rephrased only, fresh original as the bound, new information rephrased), with the repeated-data law bounding what the mixed arm can gain.
- Recursive training and collapse: a Gaussian refit simulator (`d2l.collapse_sim`) shows the tail loss under replacement and its absence under accumulation, as a mechanism under a stated protocol, not a forecast.
- Nemotron-CC-v2 and FinePhrase as the current rephrased corpora; licences of generated data and the terms check of 1.7 before an API model generates training data.
**Derivation:** none.
**Notebook:**
1. Rephrase 120 chunks live with Qwen3.5-2B (non-thinking, temperature 0.7) in both styles and inspect them; load the committed 4.7-million-token rephrased corpus generated once at authoring; report fidelity and diversity metrics against the sources and the near-duplicate rate among outputs.
2. Build the five arms from the committed files at 2^24 tokens each with the same 3.4 proxy recipe; train three data-order seeds per arm (15 runs).
3. Evaluate bits per byte on real held-out web text and on held-out rephrased text, at the end and at three intermediate points for the curves.
4. Run the collapse simulator for 20 generations under replacement and accumulation with 1,000 repetitions.
5. Claim that survives noise: the mixed arm beats both the repeated-original and the rephrased-only arms on real validation; rephrased-only loses on real validation and wins on rephrased validation (a distribution gap). Each claim needs all three seed-paired differences of one sign and a mean above two pooled standard deviations (10 degrees of freedom); a null result at this scale is written up as such, and the repeated-data law bounds the expected mixed-arm gain in advance.
6. Reused pieces: `d2l.load_hf` (1.1), `d2l.shingles`, `d2l.minhash_signature`, `d2l.lsh_candidates`, `d2l.union_find_clusters` (3.3), `d2l.train_proxy_lm`, `d2l.ProxyCfg`, `d2l.bpb_from_nll`, `d2l.effective_tokens` and the pools of 3.4, the reference tokenizer of 3.6; Transformers' batched `generate` (not `d2l.generate`, for stopping and batching, said in one sentence); new `d2l.REPHRASE_PROMPTS`, `d2l.rephrase_docs`, `d2l.synth_metrics`, `d2l.distinct_n`, `d2l.collapse_sim`, `d2l.chunk_doc`.
**Figures:** `fig_lm_rephrase_tradeoff` (plot: bits per byte on real and on rephrased validation per arm, three seed points and the mean with the pooled noise band; a second panel of real-validation curves against tokens for the repeated, mixed and fresh arms with repeat boundaries marked).
**Restate / forward:** `sec_environment-and-distribution-shift` (training on model outputs shifts the data distribution toward the generator's). Forward: 7.3 (instruction data generated by a model, where the same fidelity and licence checks return).
**Exercises:** 5 to 7. Load-bearing: measure distinct 4-gram fraction and the near-duplicate rate of original against rephrased text as a function of sampling temperature (measure); derive the rephrasing budget: the generation FLOPs per token of rephrased text against the training FLOPs it replaces, as a function of generator and student size (derive); check that no `<think>` residue appears in the committed outputs and that `finish='length'` rows were dropped (check); open: run the collapse simulator with a fixed fraction of fresh data per generation and find the fraction at which the tail stops shrinking.
**Writer notes:**
- Models: the rephraser is Qwen3.5-2B (bf16, text-only, `enable_thinking=False`; about 3.8 GB of weights, a batch-32 KV cache kept by only 6 of its 24 layers, and about 19 MB of fixed state per sequence), with Qwen3.5-4B as the stronger rephraser for a measure exercise; the factuality judge is Qwen3.5-4B, a different model from the rephraser. The generation of 4.7 million tokens is done once at authoring (about an hour batched; measure) and committed as `lm-rephrase-fwedu-5m`, which ships as text with the FineWeb-Edu ODC-By attribution because the source is FineWeb-Edu and the rephraser is Apache-2.0 (blueprint README Decision 12).
- Arms at equal nominal tokens repeat different amounts; the text compares them at equal effective tokens by `d2l.effective_tokens` as well, because eight epochs of a 2M-token slice waste a stated fraction of the nominal budget under the repeated-data law; this is why the mixed-arm gain is bounded in advance.
- Exports: `d2l.rephrase_docs(chunks, style, model_id='Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B', max_new_tokens=512, batch=32, temperature=0.7, top_p=0.9, seed=0, backend='hf')` returning rows with `finish` and dropping `length` finishes; `d2l.synth_metrics(src, out)` with `copy_rate` as character 5-gram Jaccard, `num_fidelity` as the fraction of digit strings preserved, `len_ratio`; `d2l.collapse_sim(n, generations, mode='replace'|'accumulate', reps=1000, seed=0)`.
- Pitfalls to state: judging a rephrase by fluency (a fluent paragraph can change a number); letting the generator see the benchmark (check outputs with 3.3's index); reporting only real-validation loss; one temperature and one prompt collapse diversity; the Gaussian toy is a mechanism, not a forecast; generated text is not licence-free, and the statements on licences are descriptive, not legal advice.
- Nemotron-CC-v2 and FinePhrase are the named exemplars rather than Cosmopedia (Cosmopedia is an inspection set only); the collapse paragraph and simulator are included; the rephraser is Qwen3.5-2B. Nemotron-CC-v2 is under the NVIDIA Data Agreement for Model Training, so it is cited and streamed for inspection, never shipped.
- Memory and time: 15 proxy runs at 2^24 tokens fit about 12 to 15 minutes on the build box; the scale knob multiplies the slice and the budget for a T2 variant, which requires regenerating the committed rephrase.
- Committed artifacts: `lm-rephrase-fwedu-5m` (source and rephrased token files per split, `rephrase.jsonl` with per-chunk metadata and text), the regeneration script with the prompt hash and generator revision, the manifest stage record `rephrase`.
- Do not re-teach: sampling and temperature (`sec_decoding`), the repeated-data law beyond its one formula (`sec_scaling-laws`), the generation loop (`sec_kv-cache`).
