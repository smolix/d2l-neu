# Fine-Tuning
:label:`chap_lm_finetuning`

The reader has a pretrained model that continues text and a task it should do instead: follow an instruction, keep to a format, stop when the answer is complete, classify a message. Changing some weights on a few thousand examples is the oldest route, and the questions that remain are which weights may move, in what precision, on which examples, and what the base model forgets while they move.

- *Assignment.* The chapter hosts A7 part 1 (`sec_lm_a7_sft`, T1, 8 to 12 student hours) at the end of 7.2: `render_chat` against 14 golden fixtures, the prefix invariant on 200 validation conversations, mask coverage by count, loss normalization under accumulation within `1e-6`, packing equivalence within `1e-4` with the unmasked leak detected above `1e-2`, a validation-loss band around the median of three staff runs (never narrower than 0.05 nats), an end-of-turn rate of at least 98% on 500 greedy completions, retention within the paired-bootstrap half-width, and a checkpoint that loads with `d2l.load_hf`. The token budget (default 8M supervised tokens, calibrated to 15 minutes) is the lever; a 2M-token fast path is allowed with a wider band.
- *Compute.* T1 is one 16 GB GPU at 15 GiB: a 0.6B model at 16 bytes per parameter is 9.5 GB before activations, so full fine-tuning at T1 uses sequence 512, micro-batch 4, fp32 master weights and activation checkpointing, or the 8-bit AdamW arm; LoRA notebooks fit with a bf16 base and fp32 adapters. T2 (7.1) is one A100 40 GB (about 38 GiB usable): 1.7B full fine-tuning needs 27.5 GB static, which leaves 13.3 GB, so the loss is chunked and the micro-batch is 32 rows of 128 tokens. T3 (7.6) is one 8x A100 40 GB node, run once per release. The book environment is `torch==2.15.0` on Python 3.12 with PEFT and TRL pinned together (decided 2026-10-29); Unsloth and Axolotl cap `torch` below 2.15 and torchtune pins `torchdata`, so those toolchains run from committed scripts in their own environments and are read from logs. `flash-attn` wheels may be missing for the pin; SDPA with explicit masks, then FlexAttention, are the fallbacks.
- *Cross-chapter consistency.* `d2l.chunked_lm_loss` is 4.1's and is imported, never redefined; `d2l.sft_loss` divides by the supervised-token count of the whole effective batch, computed before the first backward pass and all-reduced under DDP; the LoRA default scaling is $\alpha / r$ (PEFT's; decided 2026-10-29) with `rslora=True` as the option; the `QTensor` layout (codes packed along `in_features`, even element in the high nibble, one scale per block of 64 along the last axis) is the contract Chapters 11 and 12 read; the retention suite's task ids come from `d2l.EVAL_TASKS`; `d2l.paired_bootstrap` returns a `BootstrapResult` (`.mean`, `.ci`).
