**Idea:** A drafter that reads the target's own hidden states is better aligned than a separate model, and a prediction head trained together with the model is such a drafter that ships with it.
**Opening:** The reader has 12.4's drafter with a cost ratio near 0.15 and knows that no smaller Qwen3 sibling exists to draft for Qwen3-0.6B. The opening cell prints Qwen3-0.6B's byte ledger split into blocks (881 MB) and the tied head (311 MB), which any drafter with a full-vocabulary head pays, then the acceptance of the free n-gram drafter on chat against a passage-quoting prompt. The question is what a drafter should read and how much it may cost, and the closing question is what a head trained with the model changes.
**Teaches:**
- Three ways to draft with one verifier: a separate model; heads on the target's last feature that predict positions `t + k` independently (Medusa); a small autoregressive head that reads target features plus the sampled token (EAGLE), with EAGLE-3's multi-layer feature fusion, direct token prediction and training-time test, and a reduced draft vocabulary because the head dominates the cost for small targets.
- Tree verification: a draft tree with an ancestor mask and position ids verified in one pass through 12.1's primitive; greedy acceptance as a walk; the multi-child sampling rule (12.4's exercise) that keeps exactness; static trees from measured accuracies and dynamic trees from drafter confidence; why the right tree shrinks with batch.
- Training a drafter on the target's own generations (regenerated text, online features, a KL to the target), the data scaling it needs, and what a 20-million-token budget can and cannot show.
- Multi-token prediction as a pretraining objective: a head that predicts the next `k` tokens during training and is reused as a drafter at deployment (DeepSeek-V3's module; Qwen3.5 ships one head); teacher-forced acceptance by depth; the 2026 variants (P-EAGLE's single-pass multi-token drafter, DFlash) as one-difference pointers.
- Hybrid targets cannot roll back: a Gated DeltaNet layer folds every token into a fixed state, so a rejected suffix cannot be undone by decrementing a position; the snapshot and recompute options and their bytes.
**Derivation:** none (12.4's acceptance statistics restated; the expected accepted length of a tree is the sum of node match probabilities, so the best `N`-node tree takes the `N` most probable nodes).

**Writer notes:** Evaluate drafters on the target's own greedy or sampled continuations of held-out prompts, never on training text or teacher-forced human text; train on regenerated text, which is why EAGLE-3 regenerates.
