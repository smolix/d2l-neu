**Idea:** One sequence's attention can be split across GPUs because the softmax over a union of keys is a merge of partial results, so each GPU holds a block of the sequence and passes keys and values around a ring.
**Opening:** The reader implemented the chunked exact attention of `sec_attention-at-scale` with its three running statistics on one GPU. One concrete example: a 64k-token sequence through Qwen3-8B whose keys and values alone are 9.4 GB per layer in bf16, more than one GPU's activation budget allows for 36 layers; cutting the sequence into eight blocks puts one block on each GPU, but every query must still see every key. The open question: how partial attention results merge without changing the answer, what the ring costs per step, and how the causal mask is balanced.
**Teaches:**
- The merge of two partial attention results over disjoint key sets from their outputs and log-sum-exps, associative and commutative with an identity, safe for fully masked rows.
- Ring attention: each rank holds a query block and passes its key-value block to its neighbour c - 1 times, merging as blocks arrive; the backward needs only the output and the log-sum-exp per query, and dK, dV travel with the visiting block; what one ring step moves and when it hides behind the local block's compute.
- Causal load balance: contiguous shards give the last rank (2c-1)/c times the mean work; the zigzag partition (rank r holds chunks r and 2c-1-r) equalizes it.
- The variants and their bytes: all-gather of keys and values (simplest, largest transient memory), the ring (same bytes in c-1 steps), and the all-to-all form that trades sequence sharding for head sharding (Ulysses) with bytes smaller by a factor (1 + d_q/d_kv)/c and a head-divisibility constraint; combining context with tensor parallelism in one paragraph; positions, labels and document masks must travel with the shards.
**Derivation:** The merge and the overlap condition (blueprint ch06 6.5 D1, D3). With lse_S = log sum_{j in S} exp(a_j) and o_S the normalized output over S, the union's output is o = exp(lse_a - lse) o_a + exp(lse_b - lse) o_b with lse = logaddexp(lse_a, lse_b); the relation to the book's (m, s, o) form is lse = m + log s. A ring step overlaps iff the local block's compute of 4 (n/c)^2 d_q FLOPs takes at least as long as sending one key-value block of 2 (n/c) d_kv e bytes, that is iff n/c >= kappa eta F e d_kv / (2 beta d_q), with kappa = 2 under a causal mask; the threshold depends on the GQA ratio (four times lower for Qwen3-8B than for MHA) and on the local block length only. Smallest numeric case: two ranks, one query, two keys with logits 900 and 901 in float64, merged in both orders against the dense softmax, with the unsafe version returning NaN on a fully masked row.

**Writer notes:** Pitfalls (blueprint 6.5): `exp(-inf - -inf)` on fully masked rows is NaN, use `where(lse_new == -inf, 0, exp(lse_i - lse_new))`; contiguous causal shards; position ids not sharded with the tokens (wrong RoPE); labels shifted after sharding; document masks and position resets must travel with the shards; parameters are replicated across CP ranks and need the combined-mesh reduction; the ring with MHA at small n/c exposes communication; judging CP by memory only, since at moderate n it can be slower than FSDP plus TP. Risks: the PyTorch CP API is experimental and `set_rotate_method` lives in a private module (fallback: the all-gather and all-to-all variants as own code, the ring through the library only if the pin exposes it; the from-scratch ring still verifies correctness); offset causal masks differ per SDPA backend (fallback: FlexAttention with a position-based `mask_mod`); 128k tokens exceeds 40 GB (sequence tops out at 64k, fallback 32k); A100 runs FlashAttention-2 only. Exports: `d2l.merge_partial_attention`, `d2l.zigzag_split`, `d2l.zigzag_gather`, `d2l.ring_attention` (documented as the oracle, not the fast path, at most 16k tokens on GPU), `d2l.cp_costs`. Do not re-teach the single-device online softmax, GQA, RoPE, document masks. Detail: blueprint ch06 6.5 (D1 to D5, variants, risks).
