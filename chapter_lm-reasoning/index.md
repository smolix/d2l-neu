:label:`chap_lm_reasoning`

The reader has a 1.7B model that solves the arithmetic family after the reinforcement learning of Chapter 8, and a 0.6B model that does not. This chapter moves the capability into the smaller model, and then shows how to buy accuracy at the moment of answering with tokens instead of parameters.

**Writer notes:**
- *Running models.* Students are Qwen3-0.6B-Base and Qwen3-1.7B-Base; teachers are Qwen3-1.7B (T1) and Qwen3-8B (T2 and T3), all Apache-2.0 and all on the Qwen3 tokenizer (vocabulary 151,936). The one cross-tokenizer example is Qwen3.5-2B (vocabulary 248,320), used only to show why logit distillation needs a shared vocabulary (9.1 exercise). The trace teacher of 9.3 is Qwen3.5-4B in thinking mode (the judge model of Table 5; the blueprint's Qwen3-8B is not used): traces are text and are re-tokenized, so the vocabulary mismatch is harmless there and harmful only for logit matching. The effort-level model of 9.6 is DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 behind the API (1.7's client; recorded once and replayed at T0). Reading-only references at T1: Qwen3-1.7B in thinking mode, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B (MIT).
- *Shared artifacts and the compute unit.* 9.1 defines the chapter's compute unit, FLOPs counted from tokens by `d2l.ComputeLedger` (2 N_ne per token processed in a forward pass, generated or scored, and 6 N_ne per token trained, with N_ne the non-embedding parameter count of the model that processed the token, from `sec_scaling-laws`). Attention FLOPs are excluded and the exclusion is stated at every use. Every plot with a token or FLOP axis also reports wall-clock for the same run, because decode is memory-bound and prefill compute-bound (`sec_kv-cache`), and the text says when the two orderings disagree. When every model in a comparison is the same size the unit reduces to generated tokens, counting all generated tokens including discarded samples and thinking tokens. Teacher-trace and pool generation costs are reported as "amortized" and included in an "all-in" column.
- *Problem generator.* 9.1 exports `d2l.make_problems(family, n, seed, split, difficulty=None)` with splits `train`, `test_seen_templates`, `test_unseen_templates`, `test_harder`; the `chain` family has 24 templates and 2 to 6 reasoning steps (templates 0 to 15 train; 16 to 23 unseen; `test_harder` is templates 0 to 15 with 7 or 8 steps). Items carry `question`, `prompt` and `answer` so they pass straight to `d2l.grpo_train` as `tasks`. Every training experiment in the chapter uses these generated families (the idea of GSM-Symbolic; the notebook builds its own generator and does not redistribute `apple/GSM-Symbolic`, whose card is CC-BY-NC-ND-4.0). Public sets (GSM8K test, MATH-500, AIME 2024 and 2025) appear only as "public, possibly seen" reality checks with paired, within-model comparisons; Qwen3 was trained after they were public.
- *Formats and templates.* Both models in every pair render prompts with 7.2's `d2l_chatml_v1` (`d2l.render_chat`), so teacher and student see identical token ids; Qwen's own template is used only when a teacher generates offline samples, and those are stored without the empty `<think></think>` block that Qwen3's non-thinking template inserts. `<think>...</think>` is a response-text convention of this chapter (7.2 keeps think blocks out of `d2l_chatml_v1`); the answer format is `#### <integer>`, taught by a short SFT warm-up (9.2's S0) and checked by `d2l.format_reward` (9.3). `d2l.verify_math` (8.4) composes 1.4's `d2l.extract_answer(kind='boxed')` with `d2l.canonical_answer`.

- *Compute notes.* T1 means two scheduler slots and at most 15 GiB: the 9.1 training cell never loads the teacher (it trains from the cached top-k file); 9.2's T1 teacher scoring fits only with rollout batches of at most 32 and responses of at most 256 tokens, which the config enforces. T2 is one A100 40 GB with a target of at most 38 GiB: 9.2's memory plan is a LoRA (rank 16) on a frozen bf16 1.7B student (3.44 GB) plus a second student copy in the rollout engine plus the 8B teacher (16.38 GB), about 23.3 GB resident, with teacher scoring in micro-batches of at most 8 sequences and a chunked log-softmax; full fine-tuning of the student does not fit beside the teacher. 9.3's T3 job runs once per release on one 8x A100 node (three arms on two GPUs each, one rollout GPU and one training GPU, the remaining two GPUs generating teacher traces beforehand), at most about 2 hours; full fine-tuning state of the 1.7B student is 27.5 GB, so gradient checkpointing, micro-batches of a few thousand tokens and 4.1's `d2l.chunked_lm_loss` are mandatory. No section uses T4.
- *Noise discipline (chapter-wide).* Three seeds wherever a trained arm fits the tier; a difference is stated only if the paired-bootstrap 95% interval over problems (clustered by template on the unseen split) excludes zero and each arm's seed range is smaller than the gap; otherwise the table says "not resolved". T3 single-seed runs may be compared only if the difference exceeds twice the maximum seed spread measured at the T2 reduction. The `n_trials` subset noise of 9.5 is not problem-level noise and is never reported as such.
- *Cross-chapter consistency.* Chapter 8 is cited by this plan's numbers: 8.1 reward models, 8.4 RL with verifiable rewards (GRPO, the verifier, the `lm-grpo-qwen3-0p6b` and `lm-grpo-qwen3-1p7b-base` checkpoints; the blueprint calls this 8.5), 8.5 RL systems (`d2l.sync_weights`; the blueprint's 8.6). `tab_rl_notation` (owner `sec_mdp`) is the notation table; `tab_rl_notation_contract` in the blueprints does not exist. GKD's mixing coefficient is written alpha (beta is the KL coefficient in this part); the distillation temperature is written kappa (T is the horizon). N denotes test-time samples and K the training group size, never interchanged.
