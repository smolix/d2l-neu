**Idea:** A reasoning model is made by cold-start supervised fine-tuning on traces, reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards and distillation, and each stage changes something different and measurable.
**Opening:** After RL on checkable arithmetic (8.4) the reader's base model writes longer traces and its accuracy rises; after SFT on a stronger model's traces the `<think>` format appears at once. The concrete example is two responses to the same problem from the two checkpoints, with their lengths and `d2l.behavior_stats` marker counts beside them. The open question: which of the two generalizes to templates the training never showed, at the same compute?
**Teaches:**
- The pipeline and its stages (`tab_lm_reason_pipelines`): cold-start SFT on a few thousand verified traces, RL with verifiable rewards and a format reward, rejection-sampling SFT, distillation into smaller models; what each stage's data and signal are, as stated by the sources.
- RL from a base model and the behaviours that appear (length, format, reflection markers, pass@1) against what pass@k at large k tests; the signal argument: a group of K binary rewards carries a nonzero advantage only when mixed, with probability 1 minus p^K minus (1 minus p)^K (for K equal to 8: 0.149 at p 0.02, 0.337 at 0.05, 0.832 at 0.2, 0.992 at 0.5), so a base model near zero gives all-zero groups.
- Rejection-sampling SFT as REINFORCE without a baseline (fine-tuning on correct samples has expected gradient equal to the policy gradient); GRPO differs by the group-mean subtraction (negative evidence), the division by the standard deviation and the (K minus 1)/K shrinkage.
- Prolonged RL and its plateau; reference resets; what changes with each stage (`tab_lm_reason_changes`: base, SFT, RL, SFT then RL, RFT).
**Derivation:** none in the sense of a new result; the signal probability and the RFT identity above are stated with their check cell: 200,000 simulated groups for p in {0.02, 0.05, 0.2, 0.5} and K in {4, 8, 16} within 3 standard errors, and the four-prompt verifier toy of `sec_rl_sequences` where the exact RFT gradient equals the exact policy gradient to 1e-12 and the GRPO-centered update equals (K minus 1)/K times it at K equal to 2.

**Writer notes:**
- "RL teaches reasoning" is not a measurable claim; the measured effects are length, format, marker rate and pass@1, and capability is what pass@k at large k tests (Yue et al.). A rising marker count is not evidence of a new internal algorithm; inspect responses.
- Compute accounting: equal steps or student-only FLOPs hide the teacher; the SFT arm is plotted twice (student-only, all-in dashed). The grid C is set in FLOPs from 8.4's measured ledger (to be measured).
- The format reward is cheap to hack; check that it saturates before accuracy moves. Truncation looks like failure; report the truncation rate. A base model with p near zero on `test_harder` gives no signal; select the difficulty range from the base model's measured pass rate (committed pilot) before blaming the optimizer.
- Templates: SFT arms, SFT then RL, RFT and evaluation prompts use `d2l_chatml_v1`; the RL-from-base arm uses a plain-text prompt ("Show your reasoning in `<think>` tags, then write `#### answer`") with no chat template; the teacher uses Qwen's template with thinking enabled and its traces are re-rendered. The teacher is Qwen3.5-4B (text-only load, thinking mode); if it yields too few verified traces at the T2 reduction, Qwen3-4B is the fallback. Teacher traces longer than 1,024 student tokens are capped and the dropped fraction is recorded in the all-in ledger.
- T3 budget: three arms on two GPUs each, one rollout GPU and one training GPU; full fine-tuning state 27.5 GB leaves about 10 GB per training GPU, so gradient checkpointing and chunked loss are required; if the peak exceeds 38 GiB shorten the maximum response from 1,024 to 768, then move that arm to the T2 reduction, but never give one arm LoRA and the others full fine-tuning (a confound). The RFT arm and the reset variant are cut from T3 if the budget is tight. The T3 job runs once per release through `d2l.launch`.
- Licences: Qwen3 and OpenR1 are Apache-2.0; DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B is MIT; derived weights of released reasoning models are not redistributed. Reference resets go through `d2l.grpo_train_with_reset(policy, tasks, cfg, reset_every, reward_fn=None)`; 8.4's trainer is unchanged.
- Library additions: `d2l.format_reward(completion) -> float`, `d2l.grpo_train_with_reset`, `d2l.rejection_sample(engine, problems, n, keep, reward_fn, max_new_tokens, ledger=None)` (the ledger counts all generated tokens), `d2l.behavior_stats(text) -> dict` (a marker count, not a claim about cognition), `d2l.eval_families(model_or_engine, problems_by_split, n_samples=1, k_list=(1, 8, 64), ledger=None) -> DataFrame`, `d2l.compute_matched_grid(logs, flops_grid)` (log-linear interpolation, never extrapolates).
