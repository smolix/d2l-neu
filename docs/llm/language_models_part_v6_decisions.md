## 1. Diagnosis: what is wrong with v5

Against the five criteria (SOTA accuracy, length, top-5 course usability, fit with the book, writing-guide conformance), the 13 problems that matter, most consequential first.

1. **Re-teaching what Advanced already teaches (fit).** v5 4.3 lists z-loss, QK-clip and rewind-and-skip as new (`sec_practice` practice.md:322-340, `sec_muon` muon.md:1215); 4.6 says `sec_positional-information` "only names YaRN" (it teaches and implements position interpolation, NTK-aware scaling and YaRN at positional-information.md:730-830); 8.3 claims to deliver the DPO derivation that `sec_rl_sequences` "defers" (it is derived at rl-sequences.md:210-217 with an exercise); 8.5 lists clip-higher, GSPO ratios and Dr. GRPO aggregation as new (rl-sequences.md:140-208, `subsec_pg_loss_divisor`); 14.4's sink derivation and gated attention are in `sec_what-attention-computes` and `subsec_kv-cache-sinks`; 14.1's "design table" duplicates `tab_modern-recipe` in `sec_scaling-laws`. About a third of v5's "New" bullets are restatements.
2. **Wrong or missing labels and citers (fit).** `tab_rl_notation_contract` (three uses) does not exist; the SGNS exercise owner is wrong; `sec_scaling-laws` neither loads Penn Treebank nor cites `sec_word2vec_data`; `sec_pandas` has no citation to re-point; only adam.md:1703 cites `sec_training_systems`; `sec_fine_tuning` is in the Image Models part, so the contract row is self-contradictory; five inbound citers were missed (preface:303, why-conv.md:274, cloud-instances.md:212, lstm.md:369, decoding.md:29); the `DATA_HUB['ptb']` registration that five Advanced sections need lives only in a retiring file.
3. **Tools appendix treated as retiring (fit).** It was rewritten on 2026-11-03 and Advanced links to it. v5 "absorbs" its serving, training-systems and pinning material into 1.1, 6.1, 6.8 and 13.1, which would duplicate live text and orphan the links.
4. **Section template contradicts the writing guide (conformance).** "From scratch in at most ~150 lines", a mandatory "In production" step, per-section Sources with 5 to 12 URLs, "Builds on" prerequisite lines, learning objectives and assignment blocks inside chapters, tier labels in headings, and 4 to 7 "New" bullets per section (several sections list 8 to 10 topics). The guide wants one idea per section, 2,000 to 3,500 words, restated facts, no Further Reading tails.
5. **Too many thin systems sections for any course (usability, length).** 28 of 90 sections are hardware, parallelism, compression and serving, against 3 to 6 systems lectures in CS336 and 11-711. Chapter 5 spreads one idea (every byte crosses a ladder of links) over 5.3 to 5.6; Chapter 6 has 8 sections where courses give 2 to 3 lectures; 11.4 to 11.6 and 14.7 to 14.9 are research-frontier or niche (codebooks, DFloat11, byte patching).
6. **Course gaps (usability).** No required section on safety, misuse and societal impact (224N, CS288, 11-766, COS 484 all teach it); no agent-harness assignment, no scaling-law-fit assignment, no kernel-level exercise (Triton is only named in Advanced `sec_compilation`); no project or reproduction scaffold; no token-classification task after the NLP chapters retire; the 13-week LLM path lists about 50 sections (4 per week) and the post-training path about 33 in 10 weeks.
7. **Stale exemplars (SOTA).** "Qwen3.5 is the current model" (Qwen3.6, April 2026, and Qwen3.8, August 2026, exist; the small ladder is still Qwen3.5); thinking as a binary switch (Qwen3.8 and gpt-oss expose graded effort); DeepSeek's line skips V4 (April 2026: mHC, Engram, DSA, 1M context); Gemma 3 and Gemma Scope instead of Gemma 4 (April 2026, Apache-2.0) and Gemma Scope 2; OLMo 2 checkpoints instead of Olmo 3 stage checkpoints; LLaDA-8B instead of LLaDA2.0; MI355X instead of MI455X/Helios; generic Trainium instead of Trn3 UltraServers.
8. **Research framings that are now production facts (SOTA).** NVFP4 pretraining is how Nemotron 3 was trained (25T tokens), not a simulated idea; asynchronous RL is the default at scale and the 2026 corrections disagree; MCP sits in the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation and Agent Skills (SKILL.md) is a cross-client standard with a supply-chain attack surface.
9. **Muon framed without its reality check (SOTA).** "Fantastic Pretraining Optimizers" (ICLR 2026) bounds matrix-optimizer gains at about 1.4x, roughly 1.1x at scale; v5 would let 4.2 and 4.3 imply more.
10. **Benchmark name lists in prose (SOTA, conformance).** 1.5 and 10.5 name suites that saturate within months (GPQA Diamond and MMLU-Pro are saturated, HLE numbers conflict, SWE-bench Verified was dropped for Pro). Names belong in a per-edition data file; prose teaches the lifecycle and the statistics.
11. **Library changes left implicit (fit).** 1.2 needs QK-norm, explicit `head_dim` and a saved `generate_cached`, which `d2l.GPT` lacks; 14.6 needs a hybrid class that loads Qwen3.5; the blueprint's subclass route (`Qwen3GPT`) must be stated in the plan, not discovered by the writer.
12. **Compute floor stated twice (usability).** The brief says 16 GB, the blueprints 24 GB; the judge, QLoRA headline and Qwen3.5-9B roles depend on which. Decision G2 settles it.
13. **Plan bloat (length).** 2,339 lines, of which about 40% are Sources lists and chapter teaching-source lists; a four-version change history; a 130-section "cap" with "headroom" language. v6 must be shorter than v5 while adding the course gaps, which is only possible with the compact format of §4.1.
