# English catalog audit and handoff

The English catalog contains 1,184 concept records with 1,203 distinct source references: 1,179 local Markdown/Quarto references and 24 deployed-book URL references. Local references are line locators in the current working tree; the manifest records local HEAD and hashes, and the live navigation manifest records fetch provenance. Matching a local path to a live page does not prove identical content. The six missing same-stem pages are identified in `ENGLISH_COVERAGE.md`.

A structural pass checked every local reference against fenced blocks, directive-only lines, image-caption lines, table rows, and obvious bibliography bullets: no remaining matches to those checks. This is an artifact screen, not a proof that every reference expresses its intended sense. The extractor does not establish semantic equivalence by substring alone, and many entries from the later batches require individual sense review. Sources that occur in exercises and headings are within the plan's local scope; figure captions and code are excluded.

A focused semantic spot check inspected the source lines and surrounding context for the following IDs, including polysemous terms and multiple subject areas: `bias`, `statistical-bias`, `kernel`, `kernel-function`, `return`, `precision`, `recall`, `query`, `key`, `value`, `state`, `action`, `policy`, `feature`, `label`, `hypothesis`, `premise`, `critic`, `critic-generative`, `score-function`, `target-network`, `automatic-differentiation`, `translation-invariance`, `residual`, `sample-mean`, `sample-variance`, `implicit-differentiation`, `conditional-mutual-information`, `data-processing-inequality`, `mamba`, `transition-kernel`, `mixture-of-experts`, `kv-cache`, `multi-query-attention`, `consistency-model`, `conditional-flow-matching`, `Gaussian-path`, and `forward-posterior`. IDs are case-sensitive; the Gaussian path ID is `gaussian-path`. This is a targeted sample, not a full semantic audit, and source selection for other entries remains provisional. Earlier incorrect code-cell, bibliographic, and unrelated-sense matches were corrected or removed. `normal distribution` is an alias of `gaussian-distribution`, while statistical and model-intercept bias and the two critic senses are separate records.

Common acronyms have been added as English aliases where supported by book usage. Abbreviation matching and locale assignment still require review. In particular, mathematical/engineering terms may have other senses than those selected here; the English sense is the constraint for matching. The newer batches are primarily source-occurrence candidates with independently written definitions, not expert-vetted terminology. Review every verified language term against the intended source and concept sense before exporting it for automatic constraints.

The final basic-concept check added and inspected `model`, `training`, `activation`, and `mask`. Their cited passages explicitly define how a model predicts from features, training minimizes loss, activations are outputs of the activation function, and a dropout mask keeps or suppresses entries. Together with the 38 IDs above, these make 42 concepts marked `sense_spot_checked`; the other 1,142 retain the weaker `prose_occurrence_checked` flag.

The Spanish, French, and Brazilian Portuguese shards are reconciled to the current concept inventory. Their reviewed entries and remaining proposed terms are documented in `notes/es-fr-pt.md`; most still require terminology review before automatic use.
