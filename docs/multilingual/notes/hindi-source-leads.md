# Hindi source research leads

These are discovery notes, not term-level verification. No Hindi D2L source was found in the documented inventory. The Hindi glossary requires independent teaching sources.

- Google localized topic glossaries have cached HTML in `/tmp/d2l-glossary-corpora/google-topics/*-hi.html`. The giant all-topic page returned English body text during direct retrieval; topic pages localize correctly. HTML language metadata discloses machine translation (`hi-x-mtfrom-en`). Inspect meanings and avoid literal mistranslations.
- [CSTT AI/ML English–Hindi glossary](https://www.shabd.education.gov.in/lexicon.jsp?lexicon=cstt_fund_AIML_EngHin_glossary) advertises 4,500 words, in progress. Its [expert committee page](https://www.shabd.education.gov.in/experts.jsp?glossary=cstt_fund_AIML_EngHin_glossary) names IIT and CDAC contributors. Direct requests (HTTPS with and without www, HTTP, and web open) timed out on this run. Search snippets alone do not verify entries.
- [CSTT Learner’s Glossary of Mathematics (English–Hindi), 2017](https://www.cstt.education.gov.in/sites/default/files/learners-glossary-mathematics-eng-hindi.pdf), [publisher record](https://cstt.education.gov.in/e-cart/bags-cases/learners-glossary-mathematics). Direct and web PDF retrieval timed out. Also found [English–Hindi–Marathi mathematics glossary](https://www.cstt.education.gov.in/sites/default/files/fundamental-glossary-mathematics-eng-hin-marathi.pdf), direct retrieval timed out.
- [NCERT Hindi exemplar problems](https://ncert.nic.in/exemplar-problems.php?ln=hi) links Hindi mathematics teaching chapters, including calculus, probability, statistics and linear algebra. Follow the actual PDF links and inspect pages rather than using the index as evidence for all terminology.
- IGNOU eGyanKosh: [Unit 16, simple linear regression](https://egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/58653/1/Unit16.pdf), [Quantitative Methods II, Block 4](https://egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/85663/1/Block-4.pdf), and [statistics Block 3](https://egyankosh.ac.in/bitstream/123456789/112048/3/Block-3.pdf) are promising university Hindi teaching materials. Only search excerpts were inspected during discovery; fetch and inspect full documents before assigning verified status.

Technical Hindi has both Sanskrit-derived vocabulary and English loanwords. A government glossary demonstrates formal terminology, while actual teaching prose helps select a readable preferred form. Record alternatives and the choice rather than mechanically transliterating every English term.
