# Revision Plan, 2026-10

This plan orders the work found by four reviews of Basics, Advanced, and the
Mathematics for Deep Learning appendix:

| Review | Standard |
|---|---|
| `writing-review-2026-10.md` | `docs/writing-guide.md` |
| `code-review-2026-10.md` | `docs/code-guide.md` |
| `figure-review-2026-10.md` | `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/figure-style-guide.md` |
| `slide-review-2026-10.md`, `slide-outlines-2026-10.md` | `docs/slide-guide.md` |

Text, code, figures, and slides of a chapter are revised together, because
each depends on the others: a rewritten section changes what its figures and
slides must show, and a code change changes the outputs they display. The
plan therefore has three phases: correctness fixes that stand alone,
infrastructure that changes many chapters at once, and chapter-by-chapter
revisions.

## Phase 2: Infrastructure

One change here fixes many chapters. Items that need no notebook execution
come first.

**No re-execution needed:**

1. **Slide pipeline:** resolve `:numref:`, `:eqref:`, `:cite:` in slide
   bodies; an overflow gate in `make slides`; callouts at body size; one rule
   for `r-stretch`; the small rendering defects.
2. **Figure generators:** unitless SVG sizes, a 13 pt floor, column-width
   canvases, figstyle tokens, the axis-color decision, the JS engine's
   minimum font and outlined text; then `make figures`.
3. **Prose lint:** wh-clause headings, stock phrases, and dash density as
   `tools/lint_source.py --prose` warnings (`writing-guide.md` §14).

**Needs re-capture of affected notebooks** (GPU time; Basics in four
frameworks):

4. **Plot helpers:** `d2l.plot`, `ProgressBoard`, `show_heatmaps`,
   `show_grid`, `plot_curves` (figure review, "Infrastructure Fixes").
5. **Additive library helpers:** `synchronize`, `benchmark`, `peak_memory`
   (ch. 5); `num_params`, `param_bytes`; `EMA`; `lm_perplexity`. Callers adopt
   them chapter by chapter.
6. **Structural library changes,** after the decisions above: the `Trainer`
   redesign; the ch. 9 shared-model section (`TinyLM` with identical
   initialization, `train_steps`, `evaluate`); the attention API.
