## 1. Math-appendix coverage the GAN chapter should point to, not re-derive

None of the sections below are currently referenced from anywhere in
`chapter_generative-adversarial-networks/` — see §3. This section catalogs
what exists so the rewrite can cite instead of re-derive.

### 1a. `chapter_mdl-information-theory/mdl-information-theory.md` (`sec_mdl-information_theory`)

Entropy → cross-entropy → KL, fully derived, nats throughout:

- `eq_mdl-ent_def` / `eq_mdl-diff-ent-def` — Shannon entropy $H$ and
  differential entropy $h$ (explicitly *not* the same object; $h$ is not
  coordinate-invariant, flagged at length).
- `eq_mdl-kl_def` — KL divergence definition; **Gibbs' inequality** proved
  (§"Gibbs' Inequality", uses Jensen — see §1d) — $D_{\mathrm{KL}}\ge0$,
  $=0$ iff $P=Q$.
- `subsec_mdl-gaussian-max-entropy` (`eq_mdl-gaussian-max-entropy`) —
  Gaussian maximizes differential entropy under a variance constraint,
  proved from Gibbs.
- `eq_mdl-gaussian_kl` — closed-form KL between two univariate Gaussians
  (exactly the formula a Gaussian-decoder VAE/GAN discussion would want).
- `eq_mdl-ce_def` / `eq_mdl-ce_decomp` — cross-entropy and
  $\mathrm{CE}=H+D_{\mathrm{KL}}$.
- Coding view: Kraft inequality, Shannon codes, arithmetic coding, AEP
  (`subsec_mdl-aep`), **rate–distortion** (`subsec_mdl-rate-distortion`,
  `eq_mdl-rate-distortion` — Gaussian source closed form $R(D)=\tfrac12\log(\sigma^2/D)$,
  directly relevant framing for autoencoder/VAE-adjacent generative-model
  discussion), **channel capacity** (`subsec_mdl-channel-capacity`).
- Perplexity, label smoothing, knowledge distillation, MDL
  (`subsec_mdl-mdl`).

Not a GAN-specific section, but the entropy/KL primitives every later
divergence in `mdl-divergences-distances.md` builds on.

### 1b. `chapter_mdl-information-theory/mdl-divergences-distances.md` (`sec_mdl-divergences-distances`) — the central resource

This is the section a rewritten GAN chapter should lean on hardest. It is
**explicitly framed around generative modeling** in its own intro ("A
language model compares next-token distributions... GANs compare generated
and real samples... diffusion models compare score fields"). Contents, with
what is *derived* vs. *stated*:

- **f-divergences**, `sec_mdl-f-divergences` (`eq_mdl-f-div-def`): template
  $D_f(P\|Q)=E_Q[f(p/q)]$; non-negativity proved via Jensen
  (`subsec_mdl-jensen`). Gallery table (KL, reverse-KL, $\chi^2$, squared
  Hellinger, TV, Jensen–Shannon, $\alpha$-divergence family) with generators
  and metric/symmetry properties. JS divergence derived in closed form
  (`eq_mdl-js-def`), proved bounded by $\log 2$, saturates on disjoint
  supports (flagged as the future GAN vanishing-gradient bug).
- **Fenchel duality / f-GAN**, `sec_mdl-f-gan-dual`: Fenchel–Young
  inequality (`eq_mdl-fenchel-young`, invokes `subsec_mdl-convex-conjugate`
  from `mdl-convexity.md`), then the **f-GAN variational bound**
  (`eq_mdl-f-gan-bound`) **fully proved**, with the explicit statement:
  *"The original GAN... is the special case corresponding to the
  Jensen–Shannon generator: at the optimal discriminator, the classic GAN
  value function equals $2\,\mathrm{JS}(P,Q)-\log 4$."* This is the exact
  Goodfellow-et-al. result the current `gan.md` states only informally
  ("two-sample test") — the rewrite can cite this section instead of
  re-deriving.
- **Forward vs. reverse KL**, `sec_mdl-fwd-vs-rev-kl`: mode-covering vs.
  mode-seeking, proved (`Proposition: forward KL fits moments`), with a
  worked bimodal-Gaussian numerical example. Directly cited already by
  `chapter_deep-reinforcement-learning/regularized.md` (see §2) as the
  precedent pattern to imitate.
- **Total variation & Pinsker**, `sec_mdl-tv-pinsker`: TV = half $\ell_1$
  (proved), Pinsker's inequality (`eq_mdl-pinsker`, proved), data-processing
  inequality for f-divergences (general form, proved).
- **IPMs and MMD**, `sec_mdl-ipm-mmd` (`eq_mdl-ipm-def`, `eq_mdl-mmd2`):
  IPM definition, RKHS mean-embedding derivation of MMD in closed form,
  unbiased U-statistic estimator, worked NumPy example. Directly usable for
  an MMD-GAN discussion.
- **Optimal transport / Wasserstein-1**, `sec_mdl-optimal-transport`
  (`eq_mdl-w1-primal`): the disjoint-support pathology motivating $W_1$ is
  demonstrated numerically (point masses, JS frozen at $\log2$ vs. $W_1=|d|$
  linear) — this is *the* textbook illustration of the GAN
  vanishing-gradient problem. **Kantorovich–Rubinstein duality**
  (`eq_mdl-kr-dual`, stated with weak-duality half proved, full duality
  granted with citation) is explicitly tied to **WGAN**: *"The WGAN
  :cite:`Arjovsky.Chintala.Bottou.2017` trains exactly this dual... by weight
  clipping originally, then by gradient penalties
  :cite:`Gulrajani.Ahmed.Arjovsky.ea.2017`... and by spectral normalization
  :cite:`Miyato.Kataoka.Koyama.ea.2018`, which caps each layer's Lipschitz
  constant through its largest singular value
  (:numref:`sec_mdl-svd-low-rank`)."* 1-D closed form (`eq_mdl-w1-cdf`,
  proved), Sinkhorn/entropic OT (`eq_mdl-benamou-brenier` note: the dynamic
  Benamou–Brenier form is developed later, in the dynamics chapter — see
  §1f). `sec_mdl-svd-low-rank` lives in
  `chapter_mdl-linear-algebra/mdl-svd-low-rank.md`.
- **Scores, Fisher divergence, Stein discrepancy**,
  `sec_mdl-fisher-divergence` / `sec_mdl-stein-discrepancy`
  (`eq_mdl-fisher-div-def`, `eq_mdl-stein-identity`, `eq_mdl-ksd-def`):
  score $\nabla_x\log p$, Fisher divergence, Hyvärinen score matching
  (stated, proved in the dynamics chapter — §1f), Stein's identity (proved),
  kernel Stein discrepancy (SVGD mentioned). Not GAN material per se, but
  the natural segue to ch. 17 Diffusion.
- **`sec_mdl-divergence-objective-map`** (`table` at line ~1301): an
  explicit **training-objective → idealized-divergence** table already
  rows out: maximum likelihood → forward KL; VAE/ELBO → reverse KL;
  *"original GAN with optimal discriminator → Jensen–Shannon"*; *"f-GAN
  with unrestricted optimal critic → chosen f-divergence"*; *"WGAN with a
  valid optimal Lipschitz critic → Wasserstein-1"*; *"MMD-GAN, two-sample
  tests → MMD"*; score matching/diffusion → Fisher divergence; SVGD →
  kernel Stein discrepancy. **This table is essentially the GAN chapter's
  "landscape of objectives" section, already written**, sitting one
  chapter away and currently unreferenced from ch. 16.

### 1c. `chapter_mdl-information-theory/mdl-mutual-information.md` (`sec_mdl-mutual-information`)

MI definitions/properties (`sec_mdl-mi-recap`), data-processing inequality,
estimation-is-hard (`sec_mdl-mi-hard`, McAllester–Stratos $O(\log N)$
ceiling), then **variational bounds**: Barber–Agakov (`eq_mdl-ba_bound`,
proved), **Donsker–Varadhan** (`eq_mdl-dv_bound`, proved — same convex-
duality pattern as the f-GAN bound, explicitly cross-linked), **NWJ**
(`eq_mdl-nwj_bound`, shown algebraically identical to the f-GAN KL bound),
and **InfoNCE** (`sec_mdl-infonce`, `eq_mdl-infonce_def`/`_est`/`_bound`,
fully proved, $\log N$ ceiling). Information bottleneck
(`sec_mdl-information-bottleneck`). Not GAN-specific, but the natural home
for anything about contrastive discriminators / feature-matching losses if
the rewrite goes there; also the section `chapter_deep-reinforcement-
learning/rl-sequences.md` and the RLHF material would point to for
InfoNCE-flavored objectives (see §2 — currently nothing outside the
appendix references it).

### 1d. `chapter_mdl-optimization/mdl-convexity.md` (`sec_mdl-convexity`)

`subsec_mdl-jensen` (`eq_mdl-opt-jensen`) — Jensen's inequality, the lemma
every non-negativity proof above (Gibbs, f-divergence non-negativity,
entropy-maximization) reduces to. `subsec_mdl-convex-conjugate`
(`eq_mdl-opt-conjugate`, `eq_mdl-opt-fenchel-young`) — Fenchel conjugate and
Fenchel–Young inequality, the exact machinery `sec_mdl-f-gan-dual` invokes
by name (`:numref:`subsec_mdl-convex-conjugate``). Also proximal operators
(`subsec_mdl-proximal-operators`) and the convexity-vs-neural-networks
discussion (`### Nonconvex Neural Networks`) — relevant if the rewrite
discusses why the GAN minimax game is not a convex-concave saddle point in
practice.

### 1e. `chapter_mdl-optimization/mdl-constrained-optimization-duality.md` (`sec_mdl-constrained-optimization-duality`)

Lagrangian duality goes all the way to **strong duality / Slater's
condition** (`subsec_mdl-lagrangian-duality`), **duality as a saddle
point** (`eq_mdl-opt-saddle-point` — the general minimax-equals-saddle-point
result a rewrite could cite when framing $\min_G\max_D$), sensitivity /
shadow prices, and a worked **SVM dual** (`eq_mdl-opt-svm-dual`) plus
**water-filling**. `subsec_mdl-weight-decay-duality` connects weight decay
to a norm-ball constraint — relevant if discriminator regularization
(gradient penalty, weight clipping) is framed as constrained optimization.
Note: **Fenchel conjugate duality lives in `mdl-convexity.md`, not here** —
this file's duality is Lagrangian/KKT, a different (complementary) machinery
from the f-GAN Fenchel-conjugate route.

### 1f. `chapter_mdl-probability-statistics/`

- `mdl-maximum-likelihood.md` (`sec_mdl-maximum_likelihood`): **MLE ↔ KL

- `mdl-distributions.md` (`sec_mdl-distributions`): Gaussian pdf

- `mdl-concentration-generalization.md` (`sec_mdl-concentration-
